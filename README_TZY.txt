1. ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT FEATURES
================================================================================

A. VIEW ALL ACCOUNTS
   Step 1: Open the app and log in
   Step 2: Tap the "Account" icon at the bottom navigation bar (💳)
   Step 3: Your account list will appear with all bank accounts and e-wallets
   
B. CREATE A NEW ACCOUNT
   Step 1: Go to Account page (💳 tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Add Account" or "+" button
   Step 3: Select account type (Bank Account or E-Wallet)
   Step 4: Fill in account details:
           - Account Name
           - Bank/E-wallet Type
           - Account Number/Details
           - Balance
           - Currency
   Step 5: Tap "Create Account"

C. EDIT AN EXISTING ACCOUNT
   Step 1: Go to Account page (💳 tab)
   Step 2: Tap on the account you want to edit
   Step 3: Tap "Edit" button
   Step 4: Modify details (name, balance, currency, etc.)
   Step 5: Tap "Save Changes"

D. DELETE AN ACCOUNT
   Step 1: Go to Account page (💳 tab)
   Step 2: Find the account you want to delete
   Step 3: Tap the account and look for delete/trash icon
   Step 4: Confirm deletion (warning: cannot be undone)

E. CREATE ACCOUNT GROUPS
   Step 1: Go to Account page (💳 tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Create Account Group" or "Group Accounts" option
   Step 3: Enter group name (e.g., "Daily Spending", "Emergency Fund")
   Step 4: Select multiple accounts to group together
   Step 5: Tap "Create Group"
   Step 6: Now you can see combined balance of grouped accounts

F. BANK ICONS & BANK LIST
   Step 1: When creating or editing an account, choose a bank type
   Step 2: System automatically displays bank icon
   Step 3: Select from predefined bank list
   Step 4: System displays all Local, Islamic, and Foreign banks

2. TRANSACTION & TRANSFER FEATURES
================================================================================

A. ADD A NEW TRANSACTION (Income or Expense)
   Step 1: Go to Home page (🏠 tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Add Transaction" button or "+" icon
   Step 3: Choose transaction type:
           - INCOME (money coming in)
           - EXPENSE (money going out)
   Step 4: Fill in details:
           - Amount
           - Category (auto-suggested or manual)
           - Date & Time
           - Account
           - Description/Note
   Step 5: Optional: Add receipt photo via camera or gallery
   Step 6: AI can auto-categorize if enabled
   Step 7: Tap "Save Transaction"

B. VIEW TRANSACTION DETAILS
   Step 1: Go to Home page (🏠 tab)
   Step 2: Find the transaction in the list
   Step 3: Tap on the transaction to view full details
   Step 4: See: Amount, Category, Date, Account, Photos, Notes

C. EDIT A TRANSACTION
   Step 1: Go to Home page (🏠 tab)
   Step 2: Find and tap the transaction you want to edit
   Step 3: Tap "Edit" button
   Step 4: Modify any details (amount, category, date, etc.)
   Step 5: Tap "Save Changes"

D. DELETE A TRANSACTION
   Step 1: View transaction details (see step B above)
   Step 2: Tap "Delete" button
   Step 3: Confirm deletion

E. ADD A TRANSFER (Between Your Own Accounts)
   Step 1: Go to Home page (🏠 tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Transfer" option (separate from Transaction)
   Step 3: Select:
           - From Account (source)
           - To Account (destination)
           - Amount
           - Date
           - Description (optional)
   Step 4: Tap "Create Transfer"
   Step 5: Both account balances update automatically

F. VIEW TRANSFER HISTORY
   Step 1: Go to Home page (🏠 tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Transfers" section or tab
   Step 3: Swipe or scroll through transfer records
   Step 4: Tap on any transfer to see details

G. PROCESS TRANSFER REFUND
   Step 1: View transfer details
   Step 2: Tap "Refund" button (if transfer is reversible)
   Step 3: Confirm refund
   Step 4: Both accounts are reversed (money returns to original account)

H. ADD BULK TRANSACTIONS
   Step 1: Go to AI Features (⚙️ → AIFeatures)
   Step 2: Look for "Bulk Transactions" option
   Step 3: Choose upload method:
           - Paste text/CSV
           - Upload file
   Step 4: Format transactions (system will parse them)
   Step 5: Review parsed transactions
   Step 6: Confirm bulk creation
   Step 7: All transactions added at once

I. TRANSACTION RECEIPT & OCR
   Step 1: When adding a transaction, tap "Add Photo"
   Step 2: Take photo of receipt OR select from gallery
   Step 3: Optional: System uses OCR to extract receipt details
   Step 4: Review extracted information
   Step 5: Edit if needed and save

3. BUDGET & FORECASTING FEATURES
================================================================================

A. CREATE A BUDGET
   Step 1: Go to Budget page or Settings → Budget Management
   Step 2: Tap "Create Budget" or "+" button
   Step 3: Step 1 - Select Budget Scope:
           - Choose Ledger (financial records)
           - Choose Category (specific spending type)
           - OR specific Account
   Step 4: Step 2 - Set Budget Amount:
           - Enter budget limit (e.g., RM 500/month)
           - Select cycle type: Day, Week, Month, or Year
   Step 5: Tap "Create Budget"

B. VIEW ALL BUDGETS
   Step 1: Go to Budget page (from Settings or Home)
   Step 2: See list of all active budgets
   Step 3: Each shows:
           - Budget name and amount
           - Current spending percentage
           - Color indicator (Green/Yellow/Red)

C. EDIT A BUDGET
   Step 1: Go to Budget page
   Step 2: Find budget to edit
   Step 3: Tap "Edit" button
   Step 4: Modify:
           - Budget amount
           - Cycle type
   Step 5: Tap "Save Changes"

D. DELETE A BUDGET
   Step 1: Go to Budget page
   Step 2: Find budget to delete
   Step 3: Tap "Delete" or trash icon
   Step 4: Confirm deletion

E. BUDGET CAUTION ALERTS
   What Are They?
   - ORANGE Alert (⚠): Budget at 70-99% spent
   - RED Alert (🔴): Budget over 100% spent
   
   How to See Alerts:
   Step 1: Check bottom navigation bar for colored badge
   Step 2: Badge appears on Pet icon when alert active
   Step 3: Tap to view which budgets triggered alert
   
   How to Dismiss Alert:
   Step 1: Go to Budget page
   Step 2: Find the budget with alert
   Step 3: Tap "Dismiss Alert" button
   Step 4: Alert badge disappears

F. BUDGET FORECASTING (PREDICT FUTURE SPENDING)
   Step 1: Go to AI Features (🏠 → AIFeatures or ⚙️)
   Step 2: Look for "Budget Forecasting" option
   Step 3: Select a budget to forecast
   Step 4: System analyzes your spending patterns (past 3-6 months)
   Step 5: System predicts:
           - Forecast amount you'll spend
           - Risk level (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
           - If you'll go over budget
   Step 6: View recommendations to avoid overspending

G. VIEW OVERSPEND ANALYSIS
   Step 1: In Budget Forecasting view
   Step 2: See "Risk Analysis" section
   Step 3: Shows:
           - Current month usage
           - Forecasted month usage
           - Potential overspend amount
           - Risk percentage

H. BUDGET ALERT SETTINGS
   Step 1: Go to Settings (⚙️ tab)
   Step 2: Look for "Budget Alerts" or "Notifications"
   Step 3: Configure:
           - Alert trigger percentage (default 70%)
           - Alert types (caution/exceed)
           - Notification frequency

4. SAVINGS & SAVINGS GOALS FEATURES
================================================================================

A. CREATE A SAVINGS GOAL
   Step 1: Go to Savings page (💰 tab)
   Step 2: Tap "Create Savings Goal" or "+" button
   Step 3: Choose goal type:
           - Free Saving (save flexible amount)
           - Cycle Saving (save on schedule)
   Step 4: Enter goal details:
           - Goal name (e.g., "Vacation Fund")
           - Target amount
           - End date
           - Starting amount (if any)
   Step 5: Select:
           - Source account (where money comes from)
           - Destination account (where money goes)
   Step 6: Tap "Create Goal"

B. VIEW SAVINGS GOALS
   Step 1: Go to Savings page (💰 tab)
   Step 2: See all active savings goals
   Step 3: Each goal shows:
           - Goal name and icon
           - Current amount saved
           - Target amount
           - Progress percentage
           - Days remaining

C. MAKE SAVINGS TRANSFER
   Step 1: Go to Savings page (💰 tab)
   Step 2: Find the goal you want to contribute to
   Step 3: Tap "Add to Savings" or similar button
   Step 4: Enter amount to transfer
   Step 5: Tap "Confirm"
   Step 6: Amount moves from source to destination account

D. VIEW SAVINGS DETAIL
   Step 1: Go to Savings page (💰 tab)
   Step 2: Tap on a savings goal
   Step 3: See detailed view with:
           - Goal progress
           - All transfers history
           - Linked accounts
           - Date created

E. EDIT SAVINGS GOAL
   Step 1: View savings goal details
   Step 2: Tap "Edit" button
   Step 3: Modify:
           - Goal name
           - Target amount
           - End date
           - Cycle status (if cycle saving)
   Step 4: Tap "Save Changes"

F. DELETE SAVINGS GOAL
   Step 1: View savings goal details
   Step 2: Tap "Delete Goal" button
   Step 3: Choose what to do with accumulated savings:
           - Keep in destination account
           - Move back to source account
   Step 4: Confirm deletion

5. AI FEATURES (AUTO CATEGORIZATION & SMART TOOLS)
================================================================================

A. AUTO EXPENSE CATEGORIZATION
   Step 1: Go to AI Features (look for AI icon or 🏠 → AIFeatures)
   Step 2: Tap "Auto Expense Categorization"
   Step 3: Enter transaction description/note
   Step 4: AI analyzes text and suggests:
           - Category (e.g., Food, Transport, Shopping)
           - Amount (if mentioned)
           - Confidence score
   Step 5: Review AI suggestion
   Step 6: Accept and confirm, or manually edit
   Step 7: Transaction is created

B. INTELLIGENT SAVINGS GOAL ASSISTANT
   What It Does: Helps you create achievable savings goals based on your finances
   
   Step 1: Go to AI Features
   Step 2: Tap "Saving Goal Assistant"
   Step 3: Answer questions:
           - What's your saving goal name?
           - How much do you want to save?
           - When do you want to achieve it?
   Step 4: AI analyzes your:
           - Income patterns
           - Expense patterns
           - Available cash flow
   Step 5: AI generates:
           - Feasibility assessment
           - Monthly savings required
           - Recommended accounts
           - Milestone breakdown
   Step 6: Review plan
   Step 7: Tap "Accept Plan" to create goal

C. BUDGET FORECASTING
   Step 1: Go to AI Features
   Step 2: Tap "Budget Forecasting"
   Step 3: Select budget to forecast
   Step 4: AI analyzes 3-6 months of spending data
   Step 5: Predicts:
           - Next month spending
           - Budget status (Safe/Caution/High Risk)
           - Overspend warning with amount
   Step 6: View recommendations

D. AUTO DEDUCTION (AUTOMATIC TRANSFERS)
   What It Does: Automatically transfers money on schedule (e.g., daily, weekly)
   
   Step 1: Go to Savings page (💰 tab)
   Step 2: Look for cycle saving or auto-deduction option
   Step 3: Set up auto-deduction:
           - From account
           - To account
           - Amount
           - Frequency (daily/weekly/monthly)
           - Start date
           - End date
   Step 4: System automatically transfers on schedule
   Step 5: Can be paused/resumed/cancelled anytime

E. BULK TRANSACTION UPLOAD
   Step 1: Go to AI Features
   Step 2: Tap "Bulk Transactions" or "Batch Upload"
   Step 3: Prepare data (CSV, text, or file format)
   Step 4: Upload or paste transactions
   Step 5: AI parses and extracts:
           - Date
           - Amount
           - Description
           - Account
   Step 6: Review parsed transactions
   Step 7: Tap "Import All" to create all at once

TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Where do I find a specific feature?
A: Use the table of contents above to find the feature, then follow the steps.

Q: How do I know which currency to use?
A: Go to Settings → Currency and select your country's currency.

Q: Why did I get a red budget alert?
A: You've spent more than your budget limit. Check Budget page for details.

Q: How do I stop auto-deduction?
A: Go to the savings goal with auto-deduction and tap "Pause" or "Cancel".

Q: Can I delete a transaction?
A: Yes, view transaction details and tap "Delete". This can be undone by
   going to transaction history if needed.

Q: How does AI help with budgeting?
A: AI analyzes your spending patterns and predicts future expenses to help
   you avoid overspending.

Q: What's the difference between a ledger and budget?
A: Ledger = separate financial records (like different notebooks)
   Budget = spending limit for a category or time period

Q: Can I use multiple currencies?
A: Each account can have its own currency. App will show conversions.

Q: How does the pet motivation work?
A: Pet gets healthier when you save money and reach goals,
   Pet gets sad if you exceed budgets. This gamifies finances!

Q: Where are my receipts stored?
A: Uploaded receipt photos are saved with transactions. Tap transaction
   to view attached receipt.
